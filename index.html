Import from Strava

Strava does not let a page like this connect to your account directly, so import its export file. Do it again whenever you want to catch up.

  1. On strava.com open Settings, then My Account, then Download or Delete Your Account, then Get Started.
  2. Request your archive. Strava emails a zip file.
  3. Unzip it and choose activities.csv below. Runs, rides and swims since 1 Sep 2026 are added. Repeats are skipped.

Share between phones

Type the same sync code on every phone or computer, at least 8 characters, and keep it private. Whoever has the code can see and change your data.

Back up and share offline

Move data between phones without internet or an account. Export on one phone, send the file or text through Messages, WhatsApp or email, and restore it on the other. Restoring adds to what is already there and never deletes workouts.

How this works

Plan. 32 weeks from Mon 26 Oct 2026 to race day. Base to week 12, build to 24, race-specific to 29, taper 30 to 32. Every fourth week is lighter.

Estimate. Projects your recent hard efforts to 1.9 km, 90 km and 21.1 km, then adds ocean, hills, heat and transition time. It is a guide, not a promise.

Cut-offs. Shown at 70 min swim, 5:30 swim plus bike, 8:30 finish. Those are the usual 70.3 limits. Check the Hawaii athlete guide for the official ones.
